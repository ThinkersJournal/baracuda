kind: Added: `baracuda-cuda-emit` lowers the mbarrier family and `griddepcontrol` (ROADMAP S1 slice 1)
---
`Cuda::lower_kernel` now spells `MbarInit`, `MbarArrive` (with and without `expect_tx`), `MbarCompleteTx`, `MbarWait` (`try_wait.parity` loop), `GridDepWait` and `GridDepLaunchDependents` for sm_90 or newer (`#error` guard plus `KernelInterface::requires`; bad counts and parities trap). `CopyBulk` (1-D `cp.async.bulk` completing on an mbarrier; run, alignment and 16-byte multiple checked) is spelled too. `Wgmma*`, `Tcgen05*`, `MatrixLoad`/`MatrixStore` (blocked on `Private` allocations and 16-bit float types, not yet lowered here), named and cluster barriers stay declined by name. Tests: `tests/sm90_statements.rs` (source shape, born-red against the previous `kernel.rs`, and an NVRTC `sm_90a` assemble with an `sm_80` negative control). Hopper execution is untested (the dev box is sm_89) and the mbarrier phase-parity semantics are the oracle's, not yet cross-checked on hardware (ROADMAP S6).

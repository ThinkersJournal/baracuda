kind: Added: device audit of the vendored FlashAttention-2 launcher against the attention NaN table, including window, softcap, GQA and head dims 64/128/256 (`fa2_nan_audit`)
---
Test-only. `crates/baracuda-kernels/tests/fa2_nan_audit.rs` forces the FA2 backend (f16 and bf16) and checks the E0k table on a real device (RTX 4070, sm89): a NaN query row gives that row NaN only; a NaN attended key or value poisons every row that attends it (per kv head under GQA); a `+inf` score gives a NaN row, except under a softcap, where the score is bounded and the row stays finite; a fully masked row (causal with Q > K, or outside a sliding window) is exactly zero. Measured: MHA D=64, GQA (4 q heads, 2 kv heads), D=128 and D=256, sliding window (non-causal and causal), softcap 30, and their combination: 20 of 20 pass, no divergence from the bespoke kernels. A NaN value at a position a row does not attend DOES reach that row on FA2 (UNSPECIFIED by contract, printed not asserted). UNMEASURED: ALiBi, head dims 32/96/192, shapes with Q*K >= 1M, and the FA2 backward.

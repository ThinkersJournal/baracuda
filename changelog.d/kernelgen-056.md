The rest of the project rides the project line (stamped by the gate). Measured with `DOCS_RS=1`, no CUDA, no GPU: `cargo check -p baracuda-cuda-emit -p baracuda-cuda-parse -p baracuda-cuda-seam -p baracuda-kernels-bench --all-targets` ok (not `--workspace`: the other crates do not depend on these three; CI checks the workspace); `tools/kiss-ref-diff` `cargo check --locked` ok.

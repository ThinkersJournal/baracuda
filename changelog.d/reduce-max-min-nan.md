kind: Changed: BREAKING numerics: `Max` / `Min` reductions propagate NaN; `reduce_max_to` / `reduce_min_to` too, with an infinite identity
---
`ReducePlan` `Max` / `Min` (f32, f64, f16, bf16) folded with `fmax` / `fmin`, which skip NaN: `max [1, NaN, 0, 5]` returned 5. They now propagate NaN (torch.max / min / amax / amin, and the NaN-propagating Maximum / Minimum fold of Fuel's CPU reductions): any NaN in the row gives NaN. Among equal values the first one seen is kept, so the first of `-0.0` / `+0.0` survives. The `reduce_max_to_*` / `reduce_min_to_*` primitives (`ReduceMaxTo` / `ReduceMinTo`) combined with `(a > b) ? a : b`, which dropped or kept a NaN depending on its position, and started from `-FLT_MAX` / `+FLT_MAX`, so an all `-inf` row came out `-FLT_MAX`; they now propagate NaN the same way and start from `-inf` / `+inf`. Rows without a NaN are unchanged except the all-infinite case. A call with an empty reduction axis is still declined by the launcher (status 2), as before. Affected: `baracuda-kernels` and `baracuda-kernels-sys` from 0.0.1-alpha.21 (axis kernels) and 0.0.1-alpha.46 (`reduce_*_to`) through 0.1.0-alpha.190. The backward kernels (`reduce_max_min_backward`) are not changed here. New `tests/reduce_max_min_nan.rs` (9 device tests: f32, f64, f16, bf16 axis Max and Min, plus the f32 `_to` primitives, against a closed form): 9 of 9 failed before (22 to 23 wrong rows each), 9 of 9 pass after.

kind: Fixed: attention (`flash_sdpa`, `sdpa`, additive-mask `arbmask`) turned a NaN query row into zeros; NaN and +inf now propagate
---
The row softmax of `baracuda_kernels_flash_sdpa_<dtype>_run`, `baracuda_kernels_sdpa_<dtype>_arbmask_run` and the naive `SdpaPlan` (f32, f64, f16, bf16) took its row maximum with `if (v > m) m = v`, which a NaN never wins, and read a non-finite maximum as "fully masked, write zeros". A query row holding a NaN scored all NaN, kept the maximum at `-inf`, and was written as an all-zero output row (and `lse = -inf`): the NaN disappeared, so a diverging model kept producing zeros instead of NaN. A `+inf` score (for example a `+inf` additive mask entry) also produced zeros (torch: NaN). Now a NaN or `+inf` score poisons its row: the output row, and `lse` / `attn`, are NaN, and only that row (torch.softmax inside attention). A fully masked row (every score `-inf`, no NaN) is unchanged: zeros with `lse = -inf` (the inference convention). A NaN key or value that a row attends to (weight above 0) makes that row NaN; a NaN value at a position with weight 0 (masked out, for example causal) is now ignored rather than turning the row NaN (the weighted sum skips a zero weight). Rows without a NaN are unchanged. Not changed here: `flash_decoding`, the sm89 flash kernels and the backward kernels (separate PRs), and the vendored FA2 launcher. Affected: `baracuda-kernels` and `baracuda-kernels-sys` from 0.0.1-alpha.26 (`flash_sdpa`, `sdpa`; the first published version) and 0.0.1-alpha.58 (`arbmask`; absent from the alpha.55 .crate) through 0.1.0-alpha.197. New `tests/attention_nan_rows.rs` (10 device tests: flash_sdpa f32, f64, f16, bf16 through the FFI Fuel binds, arbmask f32, f64, f16, bf16 through the FFI, and the naive plan f32, f64): 10 of 10 failed before (a NaN query row came back as zeros), 10 of 10 pass after.

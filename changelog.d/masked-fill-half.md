kind: Added: `MaskedFillPlan` and `MaskedFillBackwardPlan` accept f16 and bf16 (new 2-byte kernel; the fill value is a bit pattern)
---
`masked_fill` had kernels for f32, f64, i32 and bool only. It now has a 2-byte kernel (`masked_fill_u16`, and `masked_fill_backward_u16`) used for f16 and bf16: the select between the source and the fill runs on the raw 16-bit values, and the fill value travels as its bit pattern in the low 16 bits of `fill_bits` (`MaskedFillDescriptor::new_f16` / `new_bf16`), so `-inf`, the most negative finite value (`-65504` for f16), a NaN payload and `-0` all fill exactly and stay distinct; nothing converts through f32. The backward writes `+0` at masked positions and copies `dout` elsewhere. Device test `masked_fill_half` (the five fills above; a source with NaN payloads, infinities, subnormals and `-0`; masks of 0, 1 and other non-zero bytes; size 1, an odd length, rank 2 and 3; the backward with a sentinel proving masked cells are written): red on the old plans (`Unsupported`, 6 of 6), green on the new ones; the f32/f64/i32/bool smoke tests still pass. This is the half `MaskedFill` that Fuel's decomposed attention (`MaskedFill(-inf)` on [B, Hq, Sq, Sk] scores) needs on CUDA; Fuel binds it after it ships (GAP-413).

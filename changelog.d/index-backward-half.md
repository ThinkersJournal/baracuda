kind: Added: GatherBackwardPlan and IndexSelectBackwardPlan take f16 and bf16 (new compare-and-swap add kernels)
---
`GatherBackwardPlan` and `IndexSelectBackwardPlan` (the adjoints of `GatherPlan` and `IndexSelectPlan`: scatter-adds of the upstream gradient into `dsrc`) now accept f16 and bf16, i32 and i64 indices (ROADMAP E3; Fuel's GAP-413). Eight new symbols, `baracuda_kernels_{gather,index_select}_backward_{f16,bf16,i64idx_f16,i64idx_bf16}_run`, instantiate the existing backward kernels on `__half` / `__nv_bfloat16`; the add is the library's compare-and-swap loop on the 32-bit word holding the 2-byte cell. As with half `ScatterAddPlan`, the ORDER of additions into one cell is undefined when indices repeat (torch's CUDA `index_add_` / `scatter_add_` atomics are order-nondeterministic too), so a half result is only as reproducible as the rounding of its partial sums; an out-of-range or negative index is skipped, the shared policy of every indexing plan (`IndexBoundsPlan` detects it). Device test `indexing_backward_half` (f16 and bf16; sm89): 200 gradient rows into 3 `dsrc` rows with exactly representable partial sums (20 runs, i32 and i64 indices) for both plans, equal to an f64 accumulation rounded once to the dtype; a NaN gradient poisons its cell only and an untouched NaN cell keeps its exact bits; out-of-range and negative indices skipped: 8 of 8 pass, red on the old plans (`select` returned `Unsupported`). Not measured: sums whose partial sums are inexact (order-dependent by design, not asserted) and throughput. The CI device run is queued, not claimed.

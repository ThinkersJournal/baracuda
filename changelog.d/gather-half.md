kind: Added: GatherPlan takes f16 and bf16 sources (routed onto the 2-byte kernel)
---
`GatherPlan` (`out[..., j, ...] = src[..., index[..., j, ...], ...]`) now accepts f16 and bf16 sources, i32 and i64 indices. It routes them onto the existing 2-byte bit-copy kernels (`gather_u16`, `gather_i64idx_u16`); a gather has no arithmetic, so every value comes back bit-for-bit (NaN payloads, signalling NaN, -0, infinities, subnormals, the largest finite values). The index policy is the one f32 has: an out-of-range or negative index leaves that output cell untouched (a kernel cannot raise an error; `IndexBoundsPlan` detects it), pinned by a sentinel in the test. Before, `select` returned `Unsupported` for a half source (ROADMAP E3 #3; Fuel's GAP-413 asks for the half index ops). Device test `gather_half` (f16 and bf16; sm89): gather dim first and last on rank 2, the middle dim on rank 3, duplicate indices, a size-1 source, i32 and i64 indices, out-of-range and negative indices: 8 of 8 pass, red on the old plan (`select` returned `Unsupported`, all 8). `ScatterPlan` already took f16/bf16 (its own kernels); `GatherBackwardPlan` and `ScatterAddPlan` half are separate rows (new atomic family for the latter). The CI device run is queued, not claimed.

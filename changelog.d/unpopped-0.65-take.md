kind: **BREAKING**: take unpopped 0.65, fireline 0.14.2, fishstringer 0.15 (emit/parse/seam crates 0.65.0; `cfamily::unary_f32/_f64` return `Result`)
---
`baracuda-cuda-emit`, `baracuda-cuda-parse` and `baracuda-cuda-seam` move to 0.65.0 (the one-version exception: they track the Unpopped minor they work with); pins are `unpopped`, `unpopped-vocab`, `unpopped-convert` and `unpopped-cpu-c` `~0.65.1`, `fireline-synth` and `fireline-grammar` `~0.14.2`, `fishstringer-slang-emit` and `-parse` `~0.15.0`, and the same `unpopped` pins in `tools/kiss-ref-diff`. Unpopped 0.65.0 made `unpopped::cfamily::unary_f32` and `unary_f64` return `Result<String, SpellError>` (they panicked on `Popc`, `Clz` and `Brev` applied to a float; see the 0.65.0 entry of Unpopped's CHANGELOG.md). The emitter's two wrappers return the `Result`, and every one of the ten plan-path spelling sites plus `cuda_unary` (f32, f64, and the f16/bf16 promote-and-demote arms) maps `SpellError::IntegerBitOp(op)` to a `Decline::UnsupportedDtypeForOp` naming the op and the dtype. A real plan never reaches that mapping: `Cuda::lower` already declines these ops by name up front, so it is a backstop. Every other op spells exactly as before: a 390-line corpus (39 float ops x 5 dtypes x both bit-move flags) generated by the same function against origin/main edb26d45 (unpopped 0.64.3) is compared byte for byte, and a mutation of the `rsqrt` spelling is caught by it (line 41). The "PANIC" doc comments in `cuda.rs` and `tests/contract.rs` now say what happens. One-copy check: `cargo tree -i unpopped-vocab` shows 0.65.1 only, and `cargo tree -d` from a resolve with no lockfile lists no `unpopped*`, `fireline*` or `fishstringer*` crate twice (the only rows are `baracuda-build` and `baracuda-types`, the same workspace version listed under two feature sets, identical on main). The 1.88 MSRV build of the six declared crates passes; `fishstringer-slang-parse` 0.15 declares Rust 1.90 and is a test-only dependency of `baracuda-cuda-emit` and `baracuda-cuda-parse`, which the MSRV job does not build. Also: the `#[allow(clippy::too_many_arguments)]` on the private `tcgen05_mma` helper now carries a `reason`.

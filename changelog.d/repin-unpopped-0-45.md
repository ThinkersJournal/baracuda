kind: **BREAKING**: `baracuda-cuda-emit`, `baracuda-cuda-parse` and `baracuda-cuda-seam` move to Unpopped 0.45.12 and FireLine `fireline-synth` 0.2
---
- **Breaking:** the emitter and parser now build against `unpopped` / `unpopped-vocab` / `unpopped-cpu-c` 0.45.12 (was 0.43.2), and `baracuda-cuda-seam` against `fireline-synth ~0.2` (was `~0.1`; `fireline-grammar` stays `~0.1`). A consumer must resolve one copy of each in its graph. The MAJOR.MINOR of `baracuda-cuda-emit` and `baracuda-cuda-parse` follow the Unpopped release (versioning exception), so this change sets `baracuda-cuda-emit`, `baracuda-cuda-parse` and `baracuda-cuda-seam` to 0.45.0 itself (PM allocation; `stamp-version.py` only stamps crates at the project's current version).
- Dev-dependencies `vulkane-slang-emit` / `vulkane-slang-parse` (deprecated, re-export FishStringer) replaced by `fishstringer-slang-emit` / `fishstringer-slang-parse` 0.1.0; the four test imports follow. `tools/kiss-ref-diff` repinned to 0.45.12.
- No source change in the library crates was needed. The Unpopped statements added in 0.45 (`CopyAsync`, `AsyncCommit`, `AsyncWait`, `Collective`) are not lowered by this change.
- Lockfile also took patch bumps from an unscoped `cargo update`: cc, const-oid, itoa, libloading, log, syn, tree-sitter.

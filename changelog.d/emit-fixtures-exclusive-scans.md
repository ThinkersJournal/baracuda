kind: Added: exclusive integer Max/Min warp scans lower; decline tests use the published unpopped fixtures
---
`baracuda-cuda-emit` now lowers an exclusive integer `Max` / `Min` warp scan (`Collective { kind: Scan { exclusive: true } }`, subgroup scope): lane 0 gets the fold's identity as unpopped 0.58.4's oracle defines it, the type's least value for `Max` and its greatest for `Min` (i32, i64, u32 in the CUDA type table). Before, any exclusive scan other than `Sum`/`And`/`Or`/`Xor` was refused ("an exclusive `Max` scan has no identity"). Still refused BY NAME, never by default: a float `Max`/`Min` (its NaN rule is not pinned down) and every `Prod` (there is no `Prod` fold), both by the fold before the identity is reached. Tests `exclusive_scans`: source-shape pins (red on the old emitter: 2 of 3 failed) and, behind `nvrtc` + `device-tests`, an sm_89 NVRTC compile plus a DEVICE run of the lowered exclusive Max/Min scans for i32, i64 and u32 on a 64-thread block (two warps; a prefix holding the extreme, all lanes the extreme, all lanes the opposite extreme, ascending, descending), equal to unpopped's oracle (`run_launch_with_subgroup`) and to a hand fold: red on the old emitter (refused: "an exclusive `Max` scan has no identity"), green on the new. The float and `Prod` refusals are tracked by ROADMAP S13.

The decline tests also take the published `unpopped::fixtures` (added in unpopped 0.58.5; a dev-dependency feature `fixtures` on the workspace's unpopped ~0.59.1, no lockfile change): `fixtures_decline` runs the one checker-valid fixture of every `Stmt` variant through the emitter and pins the census: 15 variants lower, 27 are refused with a typed emitter refusal (never the checker's `ill-formed` text), 4 fixtures are modules the backend has no entry point for. A mutant that makes `GridDepWait` silently skip fails the census. Where the emitter stops a refused fixture is pinned too: 7 at the statement's own arm (MaskedLoad, MaskedStore, AtomicRmw, AtomicCas, GridDepWait, GridDepLaunchDependents, Fence) and 20 earlier (14 at the launch shape, 6 at an allocation), at the launch shape or an allocation (Wgmma*, Tcgen05*, Mma, Matrix*, Collective, Mbar*, Copy*), where the published fixture's own launch or allocation stops the emitter before the statement's arm; for those the hand-built kernels of `unlowered_statements` stay (their statement-arm text is the one proven there). `fixtures_decline` launches nothing (lowering only); the scans run on the device as above.

kind: Added: exclusive integer Max/Min warp scans lower; decline tests use the published unpopped fixtures
---
`baracuda-cuda-emit` now lowers an exclusive integer `Max` / `Min` warp scan (`Collective { kind: Scan { exclusive: true } }`, subgroup scope): lane 0 gets the fold's identity as unpopped 0.58.4's oracle defines it, the type's least value for `Max` and its greatest for `Min` (i32, i64, u32 in the CUDA type table). Before, any exclusive scan other than `Sum`/`And`/`Or`/`Xor` was refused ("an exclusive `Max` scan has no identity"). Still refused BY NAME, never by default: a float `Max`/`Min` (its NaN rule is not pinned down) and every `Prod` (there is no `Prod` fold), both by the fold before the identity is reached. Device-free tests `exclusive_scans` (red on the old emitter: 2 of 3 failed; the NVRTC sm_89 compile of the lowered i32/i64/u32 Max/Min kernels is gated behind `nvrtc` + `device-tests`).

The decline tests also take the published `unpopped::fixtures` (unpopped 0.58.5, dev-dependency feature `fixtures`; the lockfile moves unpopped and unpopped-vocab to 0.58.5): `fixtures_decline` runs the one checker-valid fixture of every `Stmt` variant through the emitter and pins the census: 15 variants lower, 27 are refused with a typed emitter refusal (never the checker's `ill-formed` text), 4 fixtures are modules the backend has no entry point for. A mutant that makes `GridDepWait` silently skip fails the census. Where the emitter stops a refused fixture is pinned too: 7 at the statement's own arm (MaskedLoad, MaskedStore, AtomicRmw, AtomicCas, GridDepWait, GridDepLaunchDependents, Fence) and 20 earlier (14 at the launch shape, 6 at an allocation), at the launch shape or an allocation (Wgmma*, Tcgen05*, Mma, Matrix*, Collective, Mbar*, Copy*), where the published fixture's own launch or allocation stops the emitter before the statement's arm; for those the hand-built kernels of `unlowered_statements` stay (their statement-arm text is the one proven there). Not run on a device: no kernel was launched, only lowered (and NVRTC-compiled for the new scans).

kind: Changed: baracuda-cuda-emit / -parse / -seam rebuilt against unpopped 0.58.0 (BREAKING: needs unpopped ~0.58.0)
---
The emitter, parser and seam crates move to 0.58.0 to match unpopped 0.58.0 (the emitter/parser exception to the one-version rule). unpopped 0.58.0 adds `Stmt::Tcgen05Alloc` / `Tcgen05Dealloc` / `Tcgen05RelinquishPermit`, `KernelTrap::TmemMisuse`, the `Tcgen05Mma` fields `m` and `d_lane`, `Wgmma` `a_regs`, and (additive) `CopyUnpack`, an FP8 `b_ty` and 8-bit canonical layouts. `baracuda-cuda-emit` lowers none of the `tcgen05` family: `Tcgen05RelinquishPermit` reaches the emitter's "not lowered by the CUDA backend yet" arm and `Tcgen05Alloc` / `Tcgen05Dealloc` are refused at the tensor-memory allocation they name (the allocation is only used by `Tcgen05Mma` / `Tcgen05Ld`, also refused); each refusal is explicit, never a default. The new decline tests use checker-valid fixtures, so the text they assert is the emitter's (mutant-checked), not the IR checker's.

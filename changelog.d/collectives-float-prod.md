kind: Added: warp collectives for float Max/Min (NaN-propagating) and Prod lower in baracuda-cuda-emit
---
ROADMAP S13. The `Subgroup`-scope `Collective` lowering in `baracuda-cuda-emit` (`kernel.rs`) refused a float `Max` / `Min` collective (its NaN behaviour was not pinned down) and every `Prod`. It now lowers both, for `Reduce` and for `Scan` (inclusive and exclusive): a float `Max` / `Min` folds per lane with a NaN test (a NaN operand is the result, else the larger or smaller; the oracle's rule that a NaN anywhere in the fold gives NaN; no vote and no extra collective; `-0.0` and `+0.0` compare equal so a signed zero is not pinned), and lane 0 of an exclusive scan is `-inf` for `Max` and `+inf` for `Min`, spelled by bit pattern (`__int_as_float`, `__longlong_as_double`); `Prod` is `*` on a float and an unsigned integer and wraps through the unsigned type on a signed integer, with lane 0 of an exclusive scan 1. The shuffles are the existing constant full mask `0xffffffffu` of the integer collectives; the IR has no mask operand. Half types are still not lowered.

Measured on the device (RTX 4070, sm_89, through `gpu-run`): 7104 lane results equal across the device, `unpopped::oracle::kernel_launch::run_launch_with_subgroup` and a hand fold, for f32 and f64 x Max, Min, Prod and for i32, i64, u32 `Prod`, each as an inclusive scan, an exclusive scan and a reduce, on NaN-in-each-warp, infinity, ascending, descending, all-equal and wrapping-integer data (the floating `Prod` data are powers of two and signs, so the fold order cannot change a value). Two mutants fail the device test (the NaN test removed from the float fold; the `Prod` identity changed to 0) and the restored code passes. NVRTC compiles all of them for sm_89. `exclusive_scans.rs` replaces its refusal test (those folds now lower); `kernel_lowering.rs` asserts a float `Max` reduce now lowers. ROADMAP S13 records it as shipped, with the open construction question for the PM (does a constant full-mask shuffle name the participants; not decided by the lane), and S12 records "UNREAD: possible hold" for the tcgen05 and mbarrier forms (the PTX sections were not readable). The CI device run is queued, not claimed.

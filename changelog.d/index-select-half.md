kind: Added: `IndexSelectPlan` accepts f16 and bf16 (i32 and i64 indices)
---
`IndexSelectPlan` now takes f16 and bf16 sources. It routes them onto the existing 2-byte bit-copy kernels (`index_select_u16`, `index_select_i64idx_u16`); a gather has no arithmetic, so every value comes back bit-for-bit (NaN payloads, signalling NaN, -0, infinities, subnormals, the largest finite values). The index policy is the one f32 has: an out-of-range or negative index leaves that output cell untouched (a kernel cannot raise an error), pinned by a sentinel in the test. Device test `index_select_half` (select dim first, middle and last; i32 and i64 indices; duplicate indices; a size-1 source; out-of-range indices): red on the old plan (`Unsupported`, 10 of 10), green on the new one; the f32/f64/i32 smoke tests still pass. Unlike `gather_rows_{f16,bf16}`, `index_select` is not graph-capture safe (its by-value 160-byte parameter block, see the note in `build.rs`); use `gather_rows` for a captured decode-shaped lookup. Documented divergence from torch, now stated on every indexing plan (f32 and half alike): torch raises on an out-of-range index, these kernels skip it and leave the output cell untouched (it may hold stale data); ROADMAP E4 tracks a device error flag that turns it into a typed error.

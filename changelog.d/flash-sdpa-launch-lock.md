kind: Fixed: the bespoke flash-attention launchers hold the SMEM-attribute lock across the launch (hdim96 fp16 `KernelInternal(2001)` under parallel tests)
---
`fa2_hdim_fanout_smoke` failed intermittently with `bespoke run: KernelInternal(2001)` (2000 + `cudaErrorMissingConfiguration`, the check after the `<<<>>>` launch) in the hdim96 fp16 tests (device run 45928213378, main 09d5431d). Measured on the device, one clock: one test per process 0 of 60 failed, the whole binary `--test-threads=1` 0 of 40, at the default thread count 8 of 40, later 9 of 60 and 7 of 60, every failure `2001`. The Phase 59c lock covered `cudaFuncSetAttribute` only, not the launch. The launchers (`baracuda_flash_sdpa.cuh`: forward, backward dQ, backward dK/dV; `baracuda_flash_sdpa_sm89.cuh`: forward and strided forward) now take `smem_attr_mutex()` before the attribute call and hold it through their launch and the launch check. Result at the default thread count: the fixed binary failed 0 of 60 (unfixed 7 of 60 on that clock), mean wall time of the whole test binary 5544 ms against 5697 ms unfixed (launches are asynchronous: the lock spans the enqueue). A mutant that moves the launch back outside the lock fails again, 13 of 60 with `2001` (unfixed 9 of 60, same clock); the fixed run and the mutant run were not on one clock, because the mutant build overwrote the fixed binary. The mechanism is not isolated beyond that. No API change. ROADMAP S16 (this) and S17 (the runtime capture failures fixed in alpha.224), and S14 records the US11531542B2 hold on wide-int carry-flag lowering. The CI device run is queued, not claimed.

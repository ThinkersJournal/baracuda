kind: Added: Tcgen05Ld, Private allocations and subgroup launches lower in baracuda-cuda-emit; U64 barriers (ROADMAP S12a-4)
---
`baracuda-cuda-emit` (kernel layer) lowers three prerequisites and the statement that needed them. A `Private` allocation is a per-thread register array (`pv<i>`). A subgroup launch is lowered when the requested range contains 32, the size of an NVIDIA warp, on a block of whole warps (a range without 32, a cluster launch and a multi-device launch are still refused by name; `Collective`, which assumed the warp, now lowers from the published fixture too). `Tcgen05Ld` (32x32b, 1 to 128 columns, a power of two, `.cta_group` not involved) lowers to `tcgen05.ld.sync.aligned.32x32b.x<N>.b32` followed by `tcgen05.wait::ld.sync.aligned`, with the lane quadrant `32 * (warp % 4)` in bits 31:16 of the tensor-memory address (PTX ISA 9.5 9.7.18.8.3) and the registers converted to the allocation's element type. An `mbarrier` object may now be a `U64` allocation (spelled `unsigned long long`, for allocations only), which is what the published `unpopped::fixtures` declare: the plain `mbarrier` statements and `Tcgen05Commit` lowered only an `I64` barrier before, so their published fixtures were refused as "has no CUDA spelling".

Runtime UNVERIFIED on hardware (RTX 4070, no tensor memory): NVRTC for `sm_100a` compiles the load kernels (1, 4, 32 and 128 columns) and `ptxas -arch=sm_100a` accepts the PTX; a corrupted shape qualifier is rejected by ptxas ("Parsing error near '.x1'") and fails the text test, so the tests can fail. No claim beyond what ptxas accepted. `tests/fixtures_decline.rs` pins the set of statements the CUDA backend refuses from the published fixtures: it changes accordingly (`Collective`, `MbarInit`, `MbarArrive`, `MbarWait`, `Tcgen05Commit`, `Tcgen05Alloc`, `Tcgen05Dealloc`, `Tcgen05RelinquishPermit` and `Tcgen05Ld` lower; `MbarCompleteTx` and `CopyBulk` are refused at their statement arm; the warpgroup and matrix statements stop at an element type with no CUDA spelling). `Tcgen05Mma` is still refused (S12a-5: its descriptors are unread). ROADMAP S12 records S12a-4 as shipped. The CI device run is queued, not claimed.

kind: `Tcgen05Mma` lowers to `tcgen05.mma.cta_group::1.kind::f16` (ROADMAP S12a-5; ptxas-checked, runtime unverified)
---
`baracuda-cuda-emit` lowers `Stmt::Tcgen05Mma` for K-major, unswizzled `Canonical` operands of `F16` or `Bf16`, an `F32` accumulator, `m = 128` and `n` a multiple of 16 from 16 to 256 (PTX ISA 9.5 Table 50), with optional negation of `A` and `B`. The 64-bit shared-memory matrix descriptors (9.7.18.4.1: address >> 4 in bits 0-13, LBO >> 4 in 16-29, SBO >> 4 in 32-45, the fixed pattern 0b001 in 46-48, no swizzle) and the 32-bit instruction descriptor (9.7.18.4.2, Table 53: dtype, atype, btype, negate, N >> 3, M >> 4) are built from the IR; `D` is the allocation's tensor-memory address plus `d_col`; `scale_d` becomes the `enable-input-d` predicate. A `Shared` allocation of `F16` or `Bf16` is declared as raw `unsigned short` bits, 128-byte aligned; an ordinary load or store of its elements stays refused. Refused by name, not built: `m = 64` or a non-zero `d_lane`, an `F16` accumulator, a `B` type that differs from `A`, MN-major, a swizzle, `RowMajor` (the oracle's layout), any other input type. No sparsity, `.ws`, collector, `cta_group::2`, `[a-tmem]` or block-scaled form is emitted. Verified: the descriptor words are recomputed in the test from the ISA's bit positions; NVRTC for `sm_100a` and `ptxas -arch=sm_100a` accept n = 16, 64 and 256 (bf16 and f16, negated); a corrupted `.kind` is rejected by that pipeline ("Unknown modifier '.kind::f17'"). RUNTIME UNVERIFIED on hardware (no sm_100 device on the lane's box). Side effect: the published `Wgmma` fixture now reaches the emitter's own `Wgmma` arm instead of failing on the 16-bit allocation spelling (still refused by name).

kind: Added: NaN regression pins for the sequence-scan kernels (`ssd_chunk_scan` and `selective_scan` forward, sort backward, cummax/cummin f16 and bf16)
---
Test-only. `ssd_chunk_scan_nan` runs `SsdChunkScanPlan` (f32, f16, bf16) on the device with a NaN in `x`, `B`, `C`, `dt` or `A` and asserts the NaN reaches exactly the outputs the recurrence implies (a column from that step on, every column from that step on, one step only, or the head) and no other batch, head or earlier step. Measured on an RTX 4070 (sm89): 18 of 18 pass on the unchanged kernel, so this row of the NaN sweep needed no fix. `selective_scan_nan` does the same for `SelectiveScanPlan` (u, delta, A, B, C, z; with and without softplus and gating; f32, f16, bf16): 21 of 21 pass on the unchanged kernel. `sort_backward_nan` runs `SortPlan` then `SortBackwardPlan` (f32, f64, ascending and descending) on inputs with two NaNs and both infinities and gradients holding NaN, +inf and -inf: NaN sorts last ascending and first descending, the indices are a permutation, and the backward scatter carries every gradient exactly: 4 of 4 pass on the unchanged kernels. `scan_cummax_nan` now also runs cummax and cummin, forward and backward, in f16 and bf16 (16 of 16 pass on the unchanged kernels).
